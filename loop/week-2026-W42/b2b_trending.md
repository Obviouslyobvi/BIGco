# B2B Trending - week-2026-W42

Summary:
- I searched 2026 Hacker News posts for problems businesses and freelancers would pay to fix. I used HN's public search API because Reddit is still blocked.
- Strongest business pain: **clients who pay late**. One thread had 50 comments, and the poster said "nobody has a good system for it". But free and cheap reminder tools already exist.
- Strongest willingness to pay: developers want a safe "credential broker" for AI agents. That's a very technical market, and a YC company is already in it.
- Neither beats the consumer idea found by Port Factory this round (see `ports.md`).
