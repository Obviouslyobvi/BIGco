# The Everyday Prize List: selling "Featured Sweepstakes" spots

**Idea:** every sweepstakes on our list is run by a company that wants more entries. We sell
them a spot at the top of the email and the web page.

**Honest timing:** start pitching at about **200 subscribers**. Below that, there's not much to sell.
Until then, collect contacts (below) so you're ready.

## What other sites charge (benchmarks)
- Sweepstakes Advantage: advanced promotions "for as little as $1.95"
- Sweeties Sweeps: $25 to $50 for high-value prizes
- Sweepgrab: $4 to publish within 24 hours
(all via the [RafflePress roundup](https://rafflepress.com/?p=1645); prices may be out of date)

So small sites charge a few dollars to $50. We start low, then raise prices as the list grows.

## Starting rate card

| Package | What they get | Price under 1,000 subs | Price at 1,000 to 5,000 subs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Featured (1 day) | Top spot in 1 email plus the web page, marked "Featured" | $25 | $75 |
| Featured week | Top spot for 5 emails | $99 | $299 |
| Founding sponsor | First 3 buyers: one free day to try it, then 50% off for 3 months | Free trial | n/a |

**Always label paid spots "Featured" or "Sponsored".** US advertising law (FTC) requires that
paid placements are clearly marked.

## Who to pitch (we already have the list)
For each sweepstakes in today's scrape, open the **official rules** and find:
1. **Sponsor**: the brand (for example "Montana Knife Company").
2. **Administrator**: often a promotion agency that runs sweepstakes for many brands. One agency
   contact can mean many sponsors. Long-running agencies include Don Jagoda Associates (since 1962)
   and Marden-Kane (since 1957) ([AgencyCompile](https://agencycompile.com/agencies/don-jagoda-associates),
   [Chief Marketer](https://www.chiefmarketer.com/topshops/marden-kane)).
3. A **marketing or partnerships email**, from the brand's contact page or the rules.

Keep a sheet with these columns:
`Date found | Brand | Prize | Ends | Administrator | Contact email | Pitched? | Reply | Paid?`

Ask Albert to add "Sponsor" and "Administrator" columns to the scraper output, so this fills itself in.

## Pitch email (you send it; bots never send email)

**Subject:** Your [Prize] sweepstakes is on our list today

```
Hi [Name],

Your [Prize] sweepstakes is in today's Everyday Prize List, a free daily email of
100+ open sweepstakes sent to people who enter sweepstakes every day.

We list it for free. If you'd like more entries before it ends on [end date], you can
make it today's "Featured" sweepstakes: top of the email and the top of our web page.

We're new, so the first 3 sponsors get one featured day free to see the results.
After that, it's $25 a day.

Want me to feature it on [date]?

[Your name]
The Everyday Prize List
coffeeshopoffice.com/prizes
[Your mailing address]
Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.
```

**Follow-up (once, 4 days later, only if no reply):**
```
Hi [Name], quick follow-up on featuring [Prize] before it ends on [date].
The free trial day is still open. Want it?
```

## Rules for pitching
- **Pitch only sweepstakes that are still open** with at least 7 days left.
- **Never promise a number of entries.** We don't have click data yet. After each featured day,
  send the sponsor a short report: emails sent, opens, and clicks on their link (Brevo shows these).
- **Cold B2B email is allowed in the US** under CAN-SPAM, as long as it's honest, includes your
  mailing address, and lets them opt out. Honor every "no".
- **Don't feature anything that needs a purchase,** or anything that looks like a scam.

## Goal for the first month
- Collect 50 sponsor contacts.
- Send 20 pitches.
- Land 3 free trials and 1 paying sponsor.
